* Ebraham sivwi Lọt * * Melkizẹdẹk Bruba kẹ Ebram * Ọke rẹ Amrafel vwọ ovie ri Shinar, Ariọk ke ovie re Elasai, Chedor-laomer kẹ ovie rẹ Elam, Tidal kẹ ovie ri Goiim, ivie nana da kpare ofovwi bru Bera ovie ri Sodọm, Birsha ovie ri Gomora, Shinab ovie rẹ Adma, Shimeba ovie ri Zeboim, kugbe ovie ri Bẹla (kọye Zoar) Enana ejobi da rhe ghwẹ koko vwẹ ushekpotọ ri Sidim (kọye urhie rẹ ughweri). Ikpe ihwivẹ soso ọye ayen ga Chedor-laomer, ẹkẹvuọvo ikpe rẹ ihwerha re, ophu de rhi mu ayen kpahọ. Vwẹ ikpe ihwẹne rẹ Chedor-laomer kugbe ivie re vẹ ọye gba obọ na da rhe re họnre Rifem vwi Ashterof-karnaim, Zuzim vwi Ham, Emim vwi Shaveh-kiria-taim, kugbe Họr vwẹ ugbenu ri Seir re te Elparan rọ hẹ ughwru rẹ aton na; ayen da rhoma rhe ri te Enmish-pat (kọye Kadẹsh) ayen da ghwọrọ aghwa rẹ Amalẹk ejobi kugbe aghwa rẹ Amọr re dia Haz-azon-tamar. Etiyin ovie ri Sodọm, ovie ri Gomora, ovie ri Adma, ovie ri Zeboim, kugbe ovie ri Bẹla (kọye Zoar), ayen da yan phia ra gbara oma vwẹ ushekpotọ ri Sidim vi Chedor-laomer ovie ri Elam, Tidal ovie ri Goiim, Amrafel ovie ri Shinar, kugbe Ariọk ovie rẹ Elasar, ivie ẹne so iyori Ushekpotọ ri Sidim na vọnre vẹ egọdọ ri mu avwrogbo, ọke rẹ ivie ri Sodọm vi Gomora vwọ djẹ re, ẹvo de she phihọ egọdọ na, iri chekọ da djẹ kpo obo ugbenu. Kọye ivwighrẹ na da ghwẹ ekuakua ri Sodọm vi Gomora reyọ, kugbe emuọre rayen ejobi ayen da yanran kpo, ayen ji mu Lọt rhe, ọmọ rẹ oniọvo rẹ Ebram, rọ dia Sodọm, vẹ eyenre rọye, ayen de kpo. Kọye ọvo vwẹ usun rẹ ayen re djẹ vabọ da rhe, re vuẹ Ebram ri Hibru na, rọ dia kẹrẹ ọhahe ri Mamre rẹ ohwo ri Amọr, oniọvo rẹ Eskol kugbe Aner, enana vẹ Ebraham kẹ obọ. Ọke rẹ Ebram vwo nyo nẹ emu omoni rọye ighuvwun, o de sun otu rẹ ofovwi rọye phia rọ nabọ yono, re ji vwiẹ vwẹ uwevwi rọye, uri gbẹ ujori, gbẹ ihwe gbẹ ẹrenre, ayen da vwọ tua ayen rhi te Dan. Vwẹ ason re ayen da ghare oma, te ọye vẹ idibo rọye, ayen de ke ayen phihọ, ayen da vwọ tua ayen re te Hobah, rọ obenu ri Damaskọs. Etiyin o de mie ayen eyenre na ejobi, ọ da reyọ Lọt omoni rọye vẹ eyenre rọye, kugbe eya na vẹ ihwo na ejobi. Ro vwo rhivwi rhe nu ro vwo phi Chedor-laomer, kugbe ivie re vẹ ọye gba obọ kparobọ nu, ovie ri Sodọm da yanran ra vwa vwẹ ushekpotọ ri Shaveh (kọye ese ushekpotọ rẹ Ovie). Melkizẹdẹk ovie ri Salẹm da vwẹ ọkuọka vi wain vwo rhe, ọye orherẹn rẹ Ọghẹnẹ ro Me Kpenu. Ọ da nẹ ẹrhovwo kẹ tanẹ, Ebruba kẹ Ebram vwẹ oma rẹ Ọghẹnẹ ro Me Kpenu, rọ ma odjuvwu vẹ akpọ; Ebruba kẹ Ọghẹnẹ ro Me Kpenu, rọ reyọ ivwighrẹ wẹn phihọ owẹ obọ. Ebram da vwẹ ẹkẹn ihwe rẹ ekuakua na ejobi kẹ. Ovie ri Sodọm da ta kẹ, Ebram, Vwẹ ihwo na kẹ vwẹ, ẹkẹvuọvo gba reyọ ekuakua na kẹ oma wẹn. Ẹkẹvuọvo Ebram da ta kẹ ovie ri Sodọm, Mi vẹ kẹ Ọrovwohwo Ọghẹnẹ ro Me Kpenu nure, ọ rọ ma odjuvwu vẹ akpọ, nẹ Me sa reyọ ogẹ rẹ oruru-u, eyẹ ufi rẹ isabatoo, eyẹ kemu kemu rọ dia ọwẹ-ẹn, wọ da rha tanẹ, Mẹvwẹ nẹrhẹ Ebram fe. Me sa reyọ emuọvo-o jokpa rẹ emu rẹ emeshare mẹ na rere, kugbe ẹkẹn rẹ ihwo ri nene uvwe, jẹn Aner, Eskol, kugbe Mamre reyọ ẹkẹn rayen.
